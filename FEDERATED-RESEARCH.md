# Federated research exchange — editorial independence

Marginal Thinking is an independent research platform. Sharpex is a distinct economic situational intelligence service. The exchange is reciprocal and read-only: no joint editorial authority, shared publication pipeline, cross-repository writes or mutual validation. Neither is an independent corroborator of facts sourced from the other or the same original release.

## Read-only discovery
- Sharpex: https://www.sharpex.com.br/research-exchange/v1/index.json ; day shards include published nonscheduled event IDs, exact dates, scope tags (ISO/MARKET/TOPIC/RISK), source names and dates, English/Portuguese summaries, and Nexus relationships with evidence class, uncertainty, status, watch criteria and supporting event IDs.
- Marginal Thinking: https://marginalthinking.org/research-exchange/v1/index.json ; monthly shards include approved canonical public research bundles only, bilingual titles and abstracts, controlled topics and geography, publication date, available canonical research and Markdown URLs. Legacy research remains discoverable through the existing archive. No internal research context or staged material is exported.
- Authorized agents prefer a pinned read-only GitHub snapshot of logicvisionai/sharpex main. Inspect content/editions/YYYY-MM-DD.md and content/nexus/YYYY-MM-DD.md, scripts/topics.mjs and scripts/risk.mjs as relevant. Source URLs are in those canonical records. No writes to Sharpex. Public feeds are optional; missing data does not block research or build.
- No network call to the partner at site build or visitor page render. Data exchange manifests are deterministic local exports, not automatically imported evidence or a live quote feed.

## Producer
Before external research for a material current topic (especially Global Macro, markets, global supply chains, energy, physical risk, conflict/security, technology and labor), screen relevant published Sharpex events and Nexus analyses from the correct information window. Record a short candidate rationale in the existing research notes or producer checks: pinned Sharpex commit, event/analysis IDs considered, dates, original source leads, which facts were independently checked, and exclusions. Use actual ID and timestamp, not a dashboard screenshot or current-day snapshot for retrospective reports. Limit retrieval to relevant shards; no forced quota. On access failure continue with primary sources and note the limitation. Do not make any new mandatory sidecar or new approval field.
- Sharpex is discovery/orientation, not primary evidence. Independently open original source(s), verify date/vintage/units/geography/definitions and competing evidence. Do not cite a Nexus inference as a measured fact. If Sharpex provides useful context, a short attributed reference to the permanent event or analysis may be included in the normal Markdown bibliography, explicitly as situational context; cite original primary evidence separately.
- No automatic article republication, copied summaries, backlinks quotas, fabricated attribution or predetermined conclusion. Marginal Thinking research retains its own question, structural depth, methods, bilingual parity, visual evidence and current QA gates. Use historical cutoff: no later-published signals in historical editions unless explicitly treated as subsequent retrospective evidence allowed by the editorial contract.
- Research may lead to new monitoring indicators in Sharpex, but Sharpex must independently verify resulting events; Marginal Thinking does not operate that newsroom.

## QA and publisher
QA checks actual primary claim support, information cutoff, source identity and lineage, independence of purported corroboration, and preservation of uncertainty when Sharpex informed the draft. Reject any circular source chain presented as independent evidence, unverified numerical claim, causal leap, or copied news digest. Existing immutable SHA-bound approvals, temporal separation, controlled taxonomy and publisher process are unchanged. No new pipeline roles. If a report does not use Sharpex, no QA requirement to cite it.
