# Global Resource Control Atlas

## Purpose

The Global Resource Control Atlas is a cumulative analytical tool of Marginal Thinking. It maps strategic resources and capabilities while keeping distinct the concepts often collapsed into a single claim of "control": physical location, legal ownership, concession or operating rights, productive capacity, physical intermediation and technology supply.

It is not a country ranking, a power score or a claim of exhaustive global coverage.

## Unit of analysis

The canonical unit is a **control record**:

**resource or capability → physical scope → controller → controller nature → control mechanism → scale → limits → evidence**

A record can describe a reserve, operating capacity, production or processing capacity, a logistics/intermediation layer or a technological capability. The same resource may appear in more than one record when different actors control different stages.

## Control is not ownership

The Atlas never treats sovereign ownership of a subsoil resource, corporate equity, concession rights, operating control, physical trading, technology supply or regulatory jurisdiction as synonyms. Large shareholdings, market shares or traded volumes do not by themselves establish ownership of an underlying natural resource.

## Geography

**Asset geography** describes where the resource, asset or operating system physically exists. **Controller jurisdiction** identifies the legal or institutional jurisdiction of the controlling actor.

Map markers show controller jurisdiction unless explicitly stated otherwise. Multi-country and global assets are not collapsed into an invented point location.

## Evidence contract

Every public record requires a stable ID, controlled resource class, explicit subject, asset geography or scope, controller and nature, control mechanism, scale statement, mechanism statement, limitation statement, last verification date and one or more public sources.

Where published Marginal Thinking research already supports the record, its research ID is retained. Direct primary sources remain attached because the Atlas is itself a structured evidence product.

## Coverage discipline

Coverage expands selectively. Absence never means a country lacks a resource or an actor lacks influence; it means the relationship has not yet been admitted to the canonical dataset. The Atlas prefers correctly scoped relationships over synthetic global completeness.

## Update and reconciliation

The Atlas participates in publication reconciliation. New research is checked for changes to reserves, ownership, concessions, operating control, production or processing capacity, physical intermediation, technology-supply dependencies, controller jurisdiction and interpretive limits.
