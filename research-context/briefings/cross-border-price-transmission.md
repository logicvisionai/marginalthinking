# Cross-Border Price Transmission

**Scope:** border-price and domestic-price transmission; reusable research context.
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-16

## Mechanisms

Import/export price indexes, producer prices and consumer prices use different baskets, weights and stages. Changes do not automatically transmit one-to-one. Contracts, invoicing currencies, inventory cycles, transport costs, firm margins and demand affect the timing and incidence.

Partner-specific terms-of-trade indexes compare export and import purchasing power for the relevant trade basket. Aggregate export/import price changes are not equivalent to a bilateral index, real trade volume, trade balance or profit.

Energy prices interact with national energy mix, regulated tariffs, tax structures, and household consumption weights. Monetary-policy conditions may be shared across a currency area while the real-income shock and fiscal cushioning differ by country. An inflation component rate and its percentage-point contribution to headline inflation are different measurements.
