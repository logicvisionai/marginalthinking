# Third-Party Sources and Notices

Marginal Thinking uses public and licensed sources for factual grounding. A citation or use as a factual source does not transfer the source's copyright or database rights to LOGV and does not place third-party material under the site's CC BY 4.0 content license.

## Publication rule

For every daily report:

1. cite the source of material facts and market data;
2. do not reproduce third-party charts, photographs, screenshots or substantial copyrighted text unless the applicable license or permission allows it;
3. prefer original LOGV visualizations built from data that may lawfully be used for that purpose;
4. where redistribution rights are uncertain, publish analysis and a citation/link rather than the underlying third-party asset;
5. preserve any attribution, notice or license required by the original data provider.

Source-specific notices should be recorded with the relevant report or dataset whenever required.
