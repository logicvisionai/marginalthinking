# Institutional editorial QA checklist

For every change to institutional copy in English or Brazilian Portuguese:

1. State a concrete subject, procedure or limitation.
2. Remove slogans that merely claim rigor, depth or sophistication.
3. Replace abstract nouns with named actors, assets, decisions or mechanisms when possible.
4. Check that the English edition reads as native research English rather than consulting language.
5. Check that the Portuguese edition reads as original Brazilian Portuguese rather than a translation.
6. Reject copy that could be pasted onto an unrelated think tank or consultancy with little or no change.
7. Keep the two editions equivalent in meaning without forcing identical syntax.
